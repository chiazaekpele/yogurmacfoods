The Ythirst Community Project

We take yogurt drinks into Lagos classrooms. Every quarter. For free.

Funded entirely by our partner subscriptions & donations — not product sales

Since 2023, we have visited public primary schools across Lagos to serve nutrient-rich yogurt drinks to children from low-income households. Many of these children arrive at school without a morning meal. Our partners make this possible.
